Our Story

The Epitome of Grandeur in the Heart of Ekala

Hotel Ramrich and Banquets, established in 2015, stands as a premier luxury banquet destination in Ja-Ela. Featuring towering classical columns, lush landscaped gardens, and magnificent interiors, we provide the perfect setting for your most prestigious events.

The full ballroom can accommodate up to 600 theatre style. Our colossal Grand Ballroom entrance is designed to impress — every corner of Ramrich whispers of refined elegance and timeless sophistication.
