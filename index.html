ΑʹThe House

Classical Grandeur in the Heart of Ekala

Towering columns, still fountains and sun-warmed stone — Ramrich was built in the spirit of the ancient Greek agora: a place made for gathering.

Since 2015, Hotel Ramrich and Banquets has been one of the premier luxury banquet destinations in Ja-Ela. Our landscaped gardens and magnificent interiors set the stage for the most prestigious occasions.

The full ballroom accommodates up to 600 guests theatre style, and the colossal Grand Ballroom entrance is designed to impress from the very first step.
